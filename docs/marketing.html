Marketing Campaign Development

During the winter of 2025, I travelled with a small group of UC Davis students and a small group of Nepali students to a remote village in Nepal. The goal was to support a small, marginalized women-led agricultural cooperative in the village to produce and market peanuts.

I was part of a subgroup focused on marketing. We performed in-depth market and target demographic analyses of both the local village and larger Nepali cities. We also created cost analyses of various value-added products (roasted peanuts, peanut butter, etc.) We included all of this information in a small booklet for the cooperative to use throughout the next couple of years. We also provided them with logos and sample social media videos for immediate marketing use.

We also created a longer-term marketing plan, which included a larger social media campaign targeted at domestic tourists interested in agro-tourism. From a PR perspective, the goal was to highlight local stories of the farmers and the traditional agricultural practices of their marginalized community. Interested tourists would be able to actually participate in a “homestay” experience, living in the field with farmers. While peanuts would not be directly sold in this process, we concluded that this approach would be more profitable long-term.
